# FlockLedger Pricing Recommendation

## Recommended Pricing Stack

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|---|---:|---|
| **Free** | **£0** | Tracking/journal for up to **8 birds** |
| **Pro Monthly** | **£5.99/month** | **Unlimited birds**, everyday AI flock help, **20 photo scans/month** |
| **Pro Annual** | **£49.99/year** | Unlimited birds, everyday AI flock help, **300 photo scans/year** + **50 bonus scans** |
| **Hatch Pack** | **£4.99** | **25 extra photo scans** |
| **Founder Annual Offer** | **£39.99 first year** | Same as Annual, for launch only |

## Headline Recommendation

**Headline price = £49.99/year**  
with **£5.99/month** as the secondary option.

## Why this pricing

- FlockLedger is positioned as a **serious hobbyist** product rather than a basic hobby tracker.
- The differentiated value sits in **AI advisory, AI egg logging, AI chick sexing, incubation logic, and meat-bird workflows**.
- The commercial model should therefore be **free core + paid premium AI layer**, not a cheap one-time unlock.
- **Annual** should be the commercial centre of gravity.
- **Monthly** should remain available for flexibility.
- **Top-ups** should be kept in the background and surfaced only when relevant.
- A **£39.99 founder annual offer** should be used at launch to accelerate first-cohort conversion.

## Customer-Facing Structure

- **Free**: up to 8 birds
- **Pro**: unlimited birds, everyday AI flock help, includes photo scans
- **Top-ups**: extra scan packs when needed

## Strategic Notes

- Do **not** expose tokens to users.
- Do **not** lead with generic “AI uses.”
- Count the expensive, visible AI actions as **photo scans**.
- Use seasonal trial logic separately from plan pricing.
